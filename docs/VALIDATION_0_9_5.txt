OBD Lab 0.9.5 — 13 November 2026

Resulting behavior
- Connect names the target control unit and changes to Connected to <unit> after connecting. It reads only that unit's fault memory and opens its live parameters. A redundant reconnect is disabled.
- The selected-unit fault-code action uses a scoped API call. Reading gearbox faults does not request engine codes or refresh a whole-vehicle report.
- Vehicle scan is independent discovery over the chosen transport. It works without opening a unit session and preserves the existing selected unit, connection, clock and diagnostic session. Each result offers Connect to open that unit.
- Live-data cards repeat at Refresh intervals of 0.25 s, 1 s or 1 min of simulation time. Pause freezes the readings with the model.
- The single-parameter control is labelled Read. Numeric Read starts repeating automatically at the displayed interval. Identity and service operations execute individually; they do not accidentally repeat clear/session commands.
- Fault/discovery replies are recorded in the trace without replacing the selected parameter response.
- The earlier speed trace, shared WLTC controls, persistent pedals, Numbers/Graphs and add-parameter features remain available.
